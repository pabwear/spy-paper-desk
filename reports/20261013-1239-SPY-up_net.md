## Limits

- One price per minute (IEX feed; its volume is a small slice of the whole market). No quotes, so the spread is a fixed cost assumption ($0.02 a share round trip + 0.5 bp slippage each side).
- When one minute reaches both the stop and the target, the stop is assumed first.
- Several models and horizons are compared, so one can look good by luck; only the locked holdout, used once, can confirm a winner.
